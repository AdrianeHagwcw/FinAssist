FinAssist Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

FinAssist is an AI-driven financial assistant designed to help users manage their expenses, budgets, and financial information. This Privacy Policy explains how FinAssist collects, uses, and protects information when users use the application.

1. Information We Collect

FinAssist may collect the following information:

  • Email address used for account authentication.
  • Information provided through Google or Facebook authentication.
  • Income information provided during financial setup.
  • Income source and income frequency.
  • Budget information.
  • Expense information entered by the user, including amount, category, description, date, payment method, and notes.

2. How We Use Information

The information collected by FinAssist is used to provide and improve the application's financial management features. This includes:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Saving financial setup information.
  • Recording and organizing expenses.
  • Calculating budget usage and remaining budget.
  • Generating financial summaries and insights.
  • Providing the features of the FinAssist application.

3. Authentication

FinAssist uses Firebase Authentication to manage user accounts. Users may authenticate using email and password, Google, or Facebook, depending on the available sign-in options.

Authentication information is processed through the respective authentication providers.

4. Data Storage

FinAssist uses Firebase Cloud Firestore to store user profile information and expense records. Access to user data is restricted using authentication and database security rules.

5. Third-Party Services

FinAssist uses third-party services to provide application functionality. These services may include:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore
  • Google Sign-In
  • Facebook Login

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. User Data and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored by FinAssist. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

User information may be retained while the user's FinAssist account remains active or as necessary to provide the application's features.

8. User Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their FinAssist account and associated personal information. Instructions for requesting data deletion are available on our Data Deletion page.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to FinAssist or its features. The updated version will be published on this page.
