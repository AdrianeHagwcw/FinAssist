FinAssist Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

FinAssist is a personal finance app that helps users keep track of their wallets, spending, bills, installments and savings goals. FinAssist is not connected to any bank or e-wallet, and it does not make or receive payments. It only keeps the records that users enter themselves.

This Privacy Policy explains what information FinAssist collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

FinAssist collects the information users enter or allow:

  • Account information: email address and name, and the basic profile details Google shares when a user signs in with Google.
  • Financial setup and preferences: income source, how often pay comes, an optional usual income amount, an optional daily spending limit, the preferred choice for leftover money, and the expense categories a user hides.
  • Wallets: the name, type (such as cash, GCash, Maya or bank) and balance of each wallet the user adds.
  • Transactions: income, expenses and transfers between the user's own wallets, including amount, category, description, date, wallet and notes.
  • Bills and bill payments, installments, debts and money lent (including the name of the person a user types in), savings goals and contributions, savings set aside, and pay-cycle history.

2. Receipt Scanning and Voice Entry

  • Scan Receipt: photos a user takes or picks are read on the phone using Google ML Kit text recognition. The photos are not uploaded and are not saved to the user's account. Only the details the user checks and saves on the expense form are stored.
  • Voice Entry: speech is turned into text by the phone's speech recognition service (on most Android phones, provided by Google), which may process the audio online under its own privacy policy. FinAssist receives only the text, and nothing is saved until the user confirms the expense form.
  • The camera and microphone are used only while the user is using these features.

3. How We Use Information

The information is used only to provide FinAssist's features:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Keeping wallet balances, bills, installments and goals up to date.
  • Working out Safe to Spend, reports, spending summaries and money tips.
  • Answering questions in the in-app assistant.
  • Sending the bill, goal and payday reminders a user turns on.

FinAssist does not show ads, does not include advertising or analytics tools, and does not sell user information.

4. The In-App Assistant

The assistant answers questions using the records already in the user's account, and the answers are worked out on the phone. Questions are not sent to an outside service, and the conversation is not saved; it is cleared when the chat is closed.

5. Reminders

Bill, goal and payday reminders are scheduled on the phone as notifications. A payday reminder only opens the income form; FinAssist never records income or payments on its own. Reminders can be changed in the app's Reminders screen or turned off in the phone's settings.

6. Authentication

FinAssist uses Firebase Authentication to manage user accounts. Users may sign in with an email and password or with Google. Authentication information is processed by the respective providers.

7. Data Storage

User records are stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore. Database security rules allow each signed-in user to read and change only their own records. A copy is kept on the phone so the app keeps working without an internet connection, and changes are synced when the phone is back online.

Display settings, such as the app theme and whether amounts are hidden, are kept only on the phone and are not stored in the user's account.

8. Third-Party Services

FinAssist uses the following services, which process information according to their own privacy policies and terms:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore
  • Google Sign-In
  • Google ML Kit Text Recognition (runs on the phone)
  • The phone's speech recognition service (for Voice Entry)

9. User Data and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored by FinAssist. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention

User information is kept while the user's FinAssist account is active, or until the user asks for it to be deleted.

11. User Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their FinAssist account and all of its records. Instructions are on our Data Deletion page.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when FinAssist or its features change. The updated version will be published on this page.
