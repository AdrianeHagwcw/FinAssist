FinAssist Data Deletion Instructions

FinAssist users may request deletion of their account and all of the records kept in it.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your FinAssist account and its data, send a request to:

Email: arandadriane03@gmail.com

Please include the email address you use to sign in to FinAssist so that the account can be identified.

What Is Deleted

A deletion request covers everything kept in the user's FinAssist account:

  • Account information (sign-in email and name)
  • Financial setup and preferences
  • Wallets and their balances
  • Transactions: income, expenses and transfers
  • Bills and bill payments
  • Installments, debts and money lent
  • Savings goals, contributions and savings set aside
  • Pay-cycle history

Information Kept on the Phone

Receipt photos are never uploaded, and assistant conversations are never saved. Display settings and scheduled reminders stay on the phone; they are removed by clearing FinAssist's app data or uninstalling the app.

Processing of Requests

Data deletion requests will be reviewed and processed by the FinAssist application administrators. Additional information may be requested to verify ownership of the account before deletion.
