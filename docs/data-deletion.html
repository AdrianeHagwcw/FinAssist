FinAssist Data Deletion Instructions

FinAssist allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your FinAssist account and associated data, send a request to:

Email: arandadriane03@gmail.com

Please include the email address associated with your FinAssist account in your request so that the account can be identified.

Information That May Be Deleted

A data deletion request may include deletion of information associated with the user's FinAssist account, such as:

  • Account information
  • Financial setup information
  • Budget information
  • Expense records

Processing of Requests

Data deletion requests will be reviewed and processed by the FinAssist application administrators. Additional information may be requested to verify ownership of the account before deletion.
